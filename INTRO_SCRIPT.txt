3. What We Will Do, Why It Matters, Impact (0:50 to 1:30)
Speaker 1: "Our question: do rainfall and stored water change in the same way across Bangladesh, and are those differences statistically real?"
Speaker 2: "Using NASA's rainfall and gravity-based water storage data from 2002 onward, we answer five things for each region: what is changing, where, how much, whether it's significant, and what it means."

4. Execution Strategy (1:30 to 2:10)
Speaker 4: "First, we group Bangladesh into regions that match the resolution of the satellite data. Second, we remove seasonal cycles so we see long-term change, not summer versus winter. Third, we measure trends with robust methods, Theil-Sen slopes and Mann-Kendall tests, corrected for autocorrelation, with confidence intervals. Finally, we test whether regions truly differ and check that results survive changes in start and end years."

5. Dataset Justification (2:10 to 2:40)
Speaker 2: "We chose NASA's GPM IMERG for rainfall because it covers every part of the country at fine resolution. We chose GRACE and GRACE-FO because they are the only missions that sense total stored water, including underground. Rainfall shows what arrives. Storage shows what stays. And to separate groundwater from soil moisture, we're adding NASA land-model data."
