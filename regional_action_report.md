# Bengal Hydro Detective: Regional Stakeholder Action Report

This guide translates the 2002–2026 NASA satellite data into localized historical context and actionable directives for farmers, policymakers, and citizens across Bangladesh's five macro-regions.

---

## 1. Northwest Depletion Corridor (Rajshahi & Rangpur)
**Satellite Metric:** -1.25 cm/year total water storage decline (-0.39 km³/year). Rainfall shows no detectable trend (-1.91 mm/year).

*   **Historical Meaning:** This depletion is consistent with 1980s food security policies that subsidized deep tubewells, encouraging farmers to cultivate thirsty, dry-season Boro rice on Pleistocene clay terraces that impede natural monsoon recharge. 
*   **Policymaker Mandate:** The BMDA must enforce smart-card volumetric pricing and halt unmetered deep tubewell permits. Expand surface water irrigation from the Padma and Teesta rivers by 10% by 2030.
*   **Farmer DOs:** Adopt Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) using PVC pipes to cut water use by 15–30%. Plant short-duration drought-escaping T. Aman (BRRI dhan56/57) before July 24. Shift to winter crops like BARI chhola-5 (chickpea) or BARI sarisha-10 (mustard).
*   **Farmer DON'Ts:** Do not maintain continuous standing water in Boro paddies. Do not plant three-crop continuous cereal rotations. Do not delay T. Aman transplanting past July 24.
*   **Citizen Action:** Convert upland plots to drought-hardy orchards (mango, jujube, dragon fruit). Install rooftop rainwater harvesting and excavate farm recharge ponds. Stock drought-tolerant fish (tilapia, koi) in shallow ponds.

## 2. Northeast Haor Basin (Sylhet & Mymensingh)
**Satellite Metric:** -0.56 cm/year water storage decline. Mean annual dry-season drawdown is massive (58.48 cm).

*   **Historical Meaning:** Unplanned all-weather roads and upstream sedimentation have choked the Surma-Kushiyara rivers. Surface floods destroy homes in the summer, but the water drains too fast to recharge deep subsurface storage for the winter.
*   **Policymaker Mandate:** BWDB must construct and maintain engineered submersible embankments to block early April flash floods. Dredge rivers and use the excavated silt to raise rural homestead mounds above peak flood stages.
*   **Farmer DOs:** Plant flash-flood-escaping Boro varieties (BRRI dhan110, 28, 58) to ensure harvest before mid-April. In floodplains, plant submergence-tolerant Aman (BRRI dhan51/52) that survives 14 days underwater.
*   **Farmer DON'Ts:** Do not plant long-duration Boro (BRRI dhan29) in deep Haor bowls where April floods will destroy unharvested crops. Do not expand unlined earthen irrigation canals across the Mymensingh terrace.
*   **Citizen Action:** Construct floating agricultural beds (*dhap*) using water hyacinth to grow year-round vegetables during floods. Raise pond embankments and use nylon netting to prevent fish escape.

## 3. Central Megacity & Terrace (Dhaka Division)
**Satellite Metric:** -0.47 cm/year water storage decline. Highest monsoon storage response ratio (42.2%).

*   **Historical Meaning:** Relentless year-round municipal and industrial groundwater extraction from the "Dupi Tila" aquifer formation, which is capped by impervious Madhupur clay, has created a massive metropolitan cone of depression.
*   **Policymaker Mandate:** Enforce Zero-Liquid-Discharge (ZLD) for Gazipur/Narayanganj textile factories. Mandate rooftop Managed Aquifer Recharge ("MAR") in municipal building codes to inject rainwater below the clay cap.
*   **Farmer DOs:** On the Madhupur Tract, practice contour mulching and intercrop pineapple with ginger and turmeric to conserve soil moisture. Cultivate disease-resistant BRRI dhan114.
*   **Farmer DON'Ts:** Do not irrigate peri-urban vegetables with untreated industrial effluent from the Turag or Buriganga rivers. Do not clear-cut Madhupur hillocks for monoculture, which accelerates acidic runoff.
*   **Citizen Action:** Establish urban rooftop gardens with rainwater harvesting and greywater recycling to cool buildings and reduce urban runoff.

## 4. Southwest Estuarine Delta (Khulna & Barishal)
**Satellite Metric:** No detectable trend in bulk mass (-0.05 cm/year), but severe dry-season river and soil salinity intrusion.

*   **Historical Meaning:** Reduced dry-season Ganges flow and sea-level rise are driving a brackish salinity wedge into shallow subsurface waters and polders, leaving massive tracts of agricultural land fallow in winter despite no detectable trend in bulk water mass.
*   **Policymaker Mandate:** Operate coastal polder sluice gates dynamically to trap fresh monsoon river water internally before February salinity spikes. Enforce agro-ecological land zoning to protect rice polders from shrimp farming.
*   **Farmer DOs:** Cultivate salt-tolerant Boro rice (BINA dhan10, BINA dhan8, BRRI dhan67) across suitable coastal mauzas. Grow saline-tolerant sweet potato (BARI misti alu-6) on residual moisture.
*   **Farmer DON'Ts:** Do not plant salt-sensitive BRRI dhan28 in coastal mauzas. Do not breach polder embankments for brackish tiger shrimp (*bagda*) monoculture. Do not over-pump deep subsurface storage without EC monitoring.
*   **Citizen Action:** Construct raised bed-and-trench (*Sorjan*) systems to block capillary salt rise for vegetable cultivation. Stock saline-tolerant finfish (*vetki*, *bata*) in brackish ponds. Maintain Pond Sand Filters for drinking water.

## 5. Southeast Hill Tracts (Chattogram & CHT)
**Satellite Metric:** No detectable trend in bulk mass (-0.05 cm/year), but the lowest monsoon retention in the country (only 26%).

*   **Historical Meaning:** Deforestation and short-cycle slash-and-burn (*jhum*) farming on steep Tertiary hills cause 74% of heavy monsoon rain to sheet off instantly as rapid runoff, drying up natural hill springs by winter.
*   **Policymaker Mandate:** Provide land tenure security and micro-credit so farmers can transition to multi-year Sloping Agricultural Land Technology (SALT). Legally protect Village Common Forests (VCFs).
*   **Farmer DOs:** Implement SALT contour hedgerows using nitrogen-fixing legumes every 4–6 meters to diffuse runoff and build bio-terraces. Cultivate flash-flood-tolerant BRRI dhan110 in the coastal plains.
*   **Farmer DON'Ts:** Do not practice short-fallow slash-and-burn or till root crops vertically up-and-down steep slopes. Do not expand tobacco farming in river valleys.
*   **Citizen Action:** Establish multistrata perennial fruit orchards (banana, papaya, mango). Protect headwater bamboo inside Village Common Forests to revive natural springs (*chhara*) for dry-season drinking water.
