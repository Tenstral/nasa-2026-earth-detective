Bengal Hydro Detective

Witness Reports Contradict: Where Is Bangladesh's Stored Water Going?
Witness A: Inland Northwest
FALLING WATER TABLES & STRESS

Local well observations cite dropping piezometric levels, dry suction tubewells in Rajshahi and Rangpur, and dry-season Boro rice water stress.

Witness B: Northeast & Coast
EXPANSIVE MONSOON DELUGE

Regional reports describe vast wetland inundation in Sylhet haors, severe pre-monsoon flash floods, and coastal tidal surge challenges.

★ The Question: Under the same South Asian monsoon, does total water storage decline, and do regional trends differ statistically from rainfall?
Earth Observation Data

Interrogating Dual Satellite Platforms

Combining 24-year satellite gravimetry with fine-resolution precipitation and land surface modeling
1. CSR GRACE / GRACE-FO Mascons
Total Water Storage (2002-04 to 2026-07)
  • Measurement: Vertically integrated mass anomaly (groundwater, soil moisture, surface water, biomass).
  • Footprint: Coarse spatial resolution (~300 km), strictly evaluated at macro-regional boundaries.
  • Dataset: 257 monthly solutions across 24 complete years.
2. NASA GPM IMERG V07B & GLDAS
Rainfall Ground Truth (2002-01 to 2025-09)
  • Rainfall: GPM IMERG Final Run at fine 0.1° grid serves as primary precipitation baseline.
  • Independent Partitioning: NASA GLDAS Noah 2.1 and VIC 4.1.2 simulate unsaturated soil moisture (0–2 m).
  • Quarantine: CLSM 2.2 assimilates GRACE and is quarantined to prevent circular reasoning.

Physical & Human Dimension

Partitioning, Human Exposure & Seasonal Baseline

Unpacking where the water is lost, who lives there, and how the seasons behave

Topsoil vs. Subsurface

Soil Moisture: Minor Part

NASA GLDAS Noah & VIC models show unsaturated soil moisture trends explain only -0.12 to -0.10 cm/yr (~8% to 10% of NW decline). The remaining -1.12 to -1.11 cm/yr is an unpartitioned deep subsurface and surface residual.

Socioeconomic Exposure

108.5 Million People

63.9% of Bangladesh's population (2020 GPWv4) reside in the three regions with significant total water storage decline (NW: 38.8M; Central: 45.6M; NE: 24.1M). NW cropland fraction is 74.2% (MODIS MCD12Q1).

Seasonal Storage Baseline

49–58 cm Annual Swing

Mean annual dry-season drawdown is 49 to 58 cm across all regions with no detectable trend. The ongoing storage depletion is an ongoing downward shift in the multi-decadal baseline, not an expanding seasonal drop.

Cross-Region Rigor

Calibrated Between-Region Comparisons

Paired difference testing on concurrent satellite series — reporting effect sizes only

Pairwise Effect Sizes (TWS & Rainfall)

  • NW vs. SW & SE: Northwest declines about 1.2 cm/yr faster than Southwest and Southeast (Δ = -1.20 and -1.19 cm/yr; non-overlapping single-region CIs).
  • NE & Central vs. SW & SE: Decline about 0.5 cm/yr faster than southern regions (Δ = -0.54 to -0.45 cm/yr).
  • SW vs. SE: Statistically indistinguishable (Δ = +0.01 cm/yr; 95% CI: [-0.03, +0.04]).
  • Rainfall Pairs: Zero pairs differ across the country.

Placebo Check & Overlap Precaution

• Why no p-values? A circular-shift placebo test revealed elevated false-positive rates, revealing raw p-values are overconfident. In accordance with honest reporting, exact p-values are withheld.

• Descriptive note: The NE vs. Central paired test detects a small mathematical difference (-0.07 cm/yr), but their individual 95% CIs overlap. It is treated as small and descriptive.

Epistemic Humility

Methodological Limitations & Boundary Conditions

What satellite gravimetry can and cannot claim

Observational & Spatial Constraints

  • Coarse Resolution: GRACE resolves mass at ~300 km. It is a regional satellite-derived series, not a measurement of local water tables or village tubewell lift.
  • Signal Adjacency: Neighbouring macro-regions share boundaries and signal smoothing occurs.
  • Intersatellite Step Offset: A +9.15 cm step between GRACE and GRACE-FO influences single-line regression; reporting the -1.81 to -1.25 cm/yr range ensures transparency.

Ground Truth & Causal Attribution

  • No Well Validation Yet: Satellite series have not yet been calibrated against in-situ piezometric well networks from the Bangladesh Water Development Board (BWDB).
  • Attribution Boundary: Storage decline alongside no detectable rainfall trend is consistent with intensive dry-season agricultural water extraction, but is not attributable with satellite data alone.
Detection Limits & Decision Hub

Why "No Detectable Trend" Is Not "No Change"

Understanding statistical power, temporal duration, and the interactive decision hub

Statistical Power & Minimum Detectable Slopes

"No detectable trend" reflects statistical detection limits, not proof that rainfall or southern storage has zero physical variation. Wide confidence intervals stem from high natural interannual variability in monsoon cloudbursts.

• Full 24-Year Record: NW TWS MDS = 0.32 cm/yr (high power to detect -1.25 cm/yr).
• Recent 2020–2026 Window: NW TWS MDS = 2.34 cm/yr (short window below detection threshold).
